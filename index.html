Simple Diet Tips for Fitness

Eat better, recover faster, and feel stronger without complicated plans. Focus on real food, balanced portions, and consistency.

Pre‑Workout Fuel

Eat something light with carbs and a little protein 1–2 hours before training: fruit + nuts, banana + peanut butter, etc.

Post‑Workout Recovery

Within 30–60 minutes add protein + carbs: eggs, curd, chicken, paneer, or a simple protein shake with rice or roti.

General Daily Diet

Mix vegetables, whole grains, protein, and healthy fats in your meals. Drink enough water and limit junk food.

Recovery: Rest & Repair

Recovery is where your body grows stronger. Good sleep, rest days, stretching, and managing stress are as important as the workout.

Aim for 7–8 hours of sleep, 6–7 hours of light movement or walking, and at least 1–2 full rest days per week.
